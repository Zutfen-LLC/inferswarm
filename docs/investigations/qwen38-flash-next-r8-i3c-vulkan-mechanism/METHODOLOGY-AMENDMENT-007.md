## 2. Exact emitted grammar and custody boundary

All new lines use the fixed ASCII prefix `ggml_vk_i260:v1|`, emitted through `GGML_LOG_INFO` (not generic debug/narrative output). `scripts/issue260_instrumentation.py` holds anchored full-line regexes, ordering/uniqueness and source-tree rejection. A line with an instrument-prefix near miss is an error, not absent evidence. Generic text, environment variables, and the old memory ledger cannot supply a marker. Parsing alone does **not** authenticate a process: a future producer must retain raw stderr, match exact source tree and compiled binary, and bind those bytes to a fresh exact-head dispatch before invoking these observation laws. No future producer/custody integration is implemented by this issue.

H2: `ggml_vk_i260:v1|graph|id=N|phase=begin` opens a graph. Each actual successful compute submission in `ggml_vk_compute_forward` emits `submit|graph=N|id=M|phase=submit|path=serialized` or `...|path=normal` with a process-monotonic submission ID. Across one parsed unit the parser must observe submit IDs strictly increasing in retained event order: an equal ID is the duplicate law and a lower ID after a higher one is impossible for the process-global counter; gaps between IDs are legal because other process activity may consume IDs. Only the matching serialized fence wait and reset emits `submit|graph=N|id=M|phase=wait|path=serialized|wait=success`. `graph|id=N|phase=end|compute_submits=K` records the complete submit count, including zero. The parser must require every graph's begin/end, exactly K uniquely identified compute submissions and one paired wait for each serialized submission before graph end; zero-submission graphs cannot prove H2. An event-record or generic synchronize submission cannot impersonate a compute submission. A1 requires serialized records; baseline requires normal records. Mere `GGML_VK_SERIALIZE_SUBMISSIONS` presence cannot emit the serialized marker. This observes submission serialization, **not** independent proof of cross-queue alias correctness.

H3: at `ggml_vk_create_buffer`, each real buffer receives a process-monotonic identity and the selected Vulkan memory-type index and property flags (after successful allocation, including fallback). At the `ggml_vk_create_buffer_device` decision the selected branch is recorded as one of `prefer_host`, `uma`, `disable_host_visible`, `default`, with an exact-format line `ggml_vk_i260:v1|memory|role=backend|buffer=<positive decimal>|branch=<closed literal>|type=<0..31>|flags=0x<lowercase hex>`. The `ggml_backend_vk_buffer_init_tensor` site records `ggml_vk_i260:v1|tensor|name=output.weight|buffer=B|offset=O|bytes=L|allocation_size=S` only for that exact tensor name, its containing backend allocation ID, and its checked nonzero byte span inside the Vulkan buffer. This is an exact suballocation binding, **not** a claim of a separately allocated weight; another tensor may share B. A backend-buffer free emits `memory|role=backend|event=retire|buffer=B`. The target parser requires exactly one such tensor binding preceded by a matching live backend allocation, a valid span and no prior retirement. The two `ggml_vk_ensure_sync_staging_buffer` overloads separately emit `ggml_vk_i260:v1|staging|owner=<device|context>|event=<create|retire>|buffer=<id>` on actual replacement/lifecycle transitions. Staging identities cannot count as target backend allocations; wrong owner, unknown retire, reused identity, conflicting branch, reordered target binding, altered flags, copied or duplicate markers fail closed. The baseline target must record `default` on this frozen non-UMA subject; A4 must record `prefer_host` and A5 `disable_host_visible` **at the actual allocation decision**. A branch difference alone is not causal proof: the paired baseline and intervention must also select a different target memory-type index or property flags (`memory_choice_changed`). `eDeviceLocal` in both branches, or a staging allocation alone, does not pass this contrast. Even a valid contrast supports only the tested allocation-path claim, not a blanket driver-root-cause conclusion.

The exact marker regexes, per-graph submit-count and wait-pair H2 rules, H3 allocation-before-tensor with checked suballocation and staging create-before-retire rules, and adversarial near-miss examples are executable in `tests/test_issue260_observation.py`. ## 3. H5 boundedness decision

The predecessor's `GGML_VK_DISABLE_COOPMAT` controls **KHR** extension detection at `ggml-vulkan.cpp:6599–6601`; `VK_NV_cooperative_matrix2` has a distinct `GGML_VK_DISABLE_COOPMAT2` gate at `:6606–6609` (and capability enumeration at `:7495–7498`). The subject advertises `NV_coopmat2` and the family display prioritizes coopmat2 at `:7678–7680`. Thus historical A2 is structurally dead as a cooperative-matrix-family control for this subject, regardless of the new H2/H3 instrumentation.

`GGML_VK_DISABLE_COOPMAT2` is an existing *candidate* one-factor control for advertised NV feature availability; its existence is not in dispute. The source trace through Qwen3Next (`src/models/qwen3next.cpp:203–222`), `build_lora_mm` (`src/llama-graph.cpp:1514–1542`), and `ggml_vk_mul_mat` (`ggml-vulkan.cpp:10383–10454`) shows that the projection can instead take a mat-vec route for eligible outputs of at most eight columns, with a distinct MMVQ/subgroup dispatch (`:9719–9755`, `:9833–9859`, `:10011–10032`). Other shapes reach mat-mat tile/pipeline and optional split-K/reduction (`:9490–9546`, `:9700–9708`, `:9083–9129`). The frozen request makes eight *successive greedy decisions*, not an eight-column matmul; the node's actual output dimensions and selected pipeline are not known without a new exact dispatch observation. A device-level `NV_coopmat2` enumeration (`:7678–7697`) does not establish which shader executed. A vector path could leave `GGML_VK_DISABLE_COOPMAT2` dead for this output projection just as historical A2 was dead for the advertised family. A mat-mat coopmat2 dispatch could support a *narrow* new A6, but only with paired baseline/intervention records of the same node shape and an actual changed shader family. `pipeline->name` alone is not a sufficient shader-binary provenance key, and the independent MMVQ and split-K branches do not become covered by disabling the NV extension.

The missing bounded law is a source-level output-node-to-actual-dispatch/shader binding, not absence of an environment switch. A future slice would need to retain graph/node identity, exact source/destination shapes and types, selected ordered branch, chosen pipeline's shader-family identity, and any split/reduce dispatch, then evaluate whether a **single** control affects the selected path. Implementing that observation and its admissible one-factor alternative crosses multiple distinct dispatch and shader-creation sites and would be a broader source/runtime redesign than this H2/H3-only source patch. No inference run is permitted here to resolve the uncertainty. Freezing A6 or declaring H5 covered from enumeration alone would recreate A2's dead-control error.

Therefore the prospective H5 state is `H5_DISCRIMINATOR_NOT_BOUNDED`. No A6 is minted, and historical A2 retains its dead/nonterminal status. A future *separate* source/review slice may instrument an output-layer node-to-dispatch/pipeline/shape binding, determine the actual path, and then design a specific single-factor control; that is not silently included in the present freeze.
